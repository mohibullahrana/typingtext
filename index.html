Standardized Scoring Methodology

1. Typing Speed (WPM) Standardized at 5 characters per word.
WPM = (Chars / 5) / Time (Mins)
2. Accuracy Percentage Direct character-level exact matching against passage master text.
Accuracy = (Correct / Typed) × 100
3. Marks out of 10 Balanced speed and accuracy metric (Target benchmark: 40 WPM).
Score = 0.5×(Speed) + 0.5×(Accuracy)
